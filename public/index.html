INSIDE A PRIVATE TANTRA SESSION

Two hours of guided somatic and energetic exploration.

  1. 01 /

    Check-In & Intention

    We begin with a conversation about your intentions, experiences, boundaries, and what is presently happening in your body or relationship. This gives me a sense of where to begin and what kind of exploration will best serve you.

  2. 02 /

    Opening & Grounding

    Gentle somatic practices to establish presence and awareness. We may work with breath, grounding, subtle movement, and exercises that help you recognize your body's signals of comfort, tension, openness, and resistance.

  3. 03 /

    Awakening Sensation

    Through breathwork, movement, sound, and light touch where appropriate and explicitly agreed upon, we begin exploring sensation and energy. We follow what naturally emerges.

  4. 04 /

    Kundalini Activation Journey

    An immersive guided journey integrating breath, sound, movement, and tantric energy practices. We explore the movement between activation and surrender, building intensity and returning to stillness, allowing sensations and emotions to unfold at a pace that can be integrated.

  5. 05 /

    Integration & Embodied Awareness

    We close by returning to grounding, noticing shifts in your physical and emotional state, and reflecting on what emerged. Where appropriate, I'll suggest practices you can continue between sessions.

FOR THERAPISTS & REFERRING PRACTITIONERS

When insight needs an embodied dimension.

Psychotherapy can help us understand our experiences, recognize patterns, and develop new ways of thinking and relating. Yet understanding a response and experiencing something different in the body are not always the same thing.

I welcome referrals from psychotherapists, couples therapists, sex therapists, pelvic-floor physical therapists, and other practitioners whose clients are interested in exploring somatic awareness, embodied intimacy, pleasure, boundaries, and relational practices alongside their existing therapeutic work.

Private Tantra and somatic sessions offer guided experiential practices. They are complementary to appropriate clinical care and do not replace psychotherapy or medical treatment.

Where appropriate, and with the client's explicit permission, I am open to collaborating with referring practitioners to establish shared intentions, understand relevant boundaries, and support continuity between their therapeutic work and embodied practice.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the difference between Tantra and a sexual service?+

Tantra is a tradition of working with consciousness, attention, energy, and the relationship between body and mind.

My sessions focus on somatic exploration, breathwork, movement, conscious intimacy, energetic awareness, and Kundalini practices.

There is no genital touching, sexual contact, or sexual service of any kind. The sessions do not include sexual gratification or orgasm as a service or expected outcome.

Will there be nudity or physical touch?+

I remain clothed throughout every session. You are not required to undress.

Depending on the session and your intentions, certain practices may involve light, non-genital touch. Any physical contact is discussed beforehand and requires your explicit, ongoing consent.

You can decline or stop any practice at any moment, without having to justify your decision.

How are couples sessions different?+

Couples sessions explore the relationship between two people.

I guide practices in conscious touch, breath, movement, communication, energetic awareness, and the experience of giving and receiving.

Together we cultivate awareness, connection, and new relational possibilities.

Do I need previous experience with Tantra or Kundalini?+

No. We begin with your existing level of experience, comfort, and capacity.

There is no expectation that you should know how to move energy, enter a trance, or experience a particular state.

For experienced practitioners, sessions may offer an opportunity to deepen and refine an existing practice.

Can Tantra help with painful sex or erectile dysfunction?+

Somatic and tantric practices can offer opportunities to explore relaxation, bodily awareness, boundaries, and performance-related anxiety.

Painful intercourse and erectile dysfunction can have medical and psychological causes. I encourage appropriate evaluation and welcome collaboration with pelvic-floor physical therapists, physicians, and sex therapists.

Individual outcomes vary, and these sessions are not medical treatment.

What if I have a history of trauma or I'm currently in therapy?+

You are welcome to inquire. We can discuss whether this approach is appropriate for your current circumstances, what boundaries or modifications may be important, and whether collaboration with your therapist would be helpful.

You are never expected to disclose your entire history or participate in an exercise that feels inappropriate.

Tantra sessions do not replace psychotherapy, trauma treatment, or mental-health care.

Can I book a session with my partner?+

Yes. Couples are welcome, whether you're exploring Tantra together for the first time or already have an established practice.

We begin by discussing both partners' intentions and boundaries.

Can Tantra sessions be done online?+

Private Tantra and Kundalini activation sessions are currently offered in person only.

Hypnotherapy is available online and in person.

How many sessions should I book?+

You are welcome to begin with a single experience.

Some people come with a specific intention, while others are interested in a longer progression that allows us to explore different phases of somatic awareness, intimacy, and energetic activation.

For ongoing work, monthly and twice-monthly memberships offer continuity and reduced session rates.
